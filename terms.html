What Winter Arc is

Winter Arc is a private record of a 90 day challenge you set for yourself. You write your own daily non-negotiables, you check them off, and the app keeps the record and draws it back to you. Everything it shows is arithmetic over what you entered.

What Winter Arc is not

  • It is not health, fitness, dietary, medical or psychological advice.
  • It does not know you, your health, your history or your limits.
  • It does not prescribe what your non-negotiables should be, and the suggestions it offers are prompts to edit, never instructions to follow.
  • It is not a substitute for a doctor, a coach or your own judgement about what is safe for you to do.

Decide what belongs on your own list, and stop or change anything that hurts. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, are recovering from an injury or are unsure whether a commitment is safe for you, ask a clinician before you make it one.

Your record is yours
