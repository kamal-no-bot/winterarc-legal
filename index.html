Winter Arc Support

A 90 day challenge companion for iPhone. Offline, no account.

Need help? Email support@madebykamal.app. Every message is read and answered by the developer, usually within a couple of days.

Common questions

I missed a day. Have I lost the arc?

No, and the app is built so that you cannot. A missed day is recorded as missed and the arc continues. Nothing resets, nothing is deleted, and no screen tells you that you have failed. Your run of consecutive days starts again, because that is what a run is, but the arc itself keeps every day you did.

Can I change my non-negotiables after I have signed?

You can add to them and you can retire one, and the change applies from the day you make it. It never rewrites the days before it. That is deliberate: a tracker that lets you edit the past is a tracker whose record means nothing.

Does it need internet?

No. The app contains no networking code at all. Everything is computed on your iPhone. The only network activity is Apple handling a purchase or a restore if you choose to buy.

Do I need an account?

There is no account, no sign in and no email to give. That also means there is no sync between devices in this version, and your arc lives on the iPhone you keep it on.

What stays free?

Your arc, your daily check off, your run and your share cards. Those are the app, and they are not behind a purchase.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchase.

Where are my photos kept?

In the app's own storage on your iPhone. They are not written to your photo library unless you export them yourself, they are never uploaded, and nothing analyses them.
