Privacy Policy

The short version: this app collects nothing, because it sends nothing anywhere.

What is collected

Nothing. There is no account, no sign in, no analytics, no advertising identifier and no crash reporting service.

What stays on your device

  • Your contract: the non-negotiables you wrote and when you changed them.
  • Every day of your arc, and what you checked off on it.
  • Any weekly photos you added.
  • Any note or reflection you wrote.
  • Whether you have bought the unlock.

All of it is stored in the app's own container on your iPhone, plus a small shared file that lets the widget draw your arc on the home screen. None of it is transmitted, and the developer cannot see any of it.

Network

The app contains no networking code. The only network activity is Apple handling a purchase or a restore if you choose to buy, which is Apple receiving your purchase rather than this app sending anything to the developer.

Camera and photos

You can add a photo to a week, either from your photo library or by taking one. The app asks for camera access only at the moment you choose to take a photo, and picking from your library uses Apple's own picker, which hands over the single image you chose and gives the app no access to the rest. Photos are kept in the app's own storage, are never written back to your photo library unless you export them yourself, are never uploaded, and are never analysed.

Suggestions written on your device

When you ask the app to help draft your non-negotiables, the text is produced by Apple's on-device model or, where that is unavailable, from a list bundled inside the app. Nothing you write is sent anywhere for this, and no suggestion is generated from anyone else's data.

Notifications

If you turn on a reminder, it is scheduled on your device by iOS. No reminder is sent from a server, and their contents never leave your iPhone.

Share cards

Share cards are drawn on your device. They carry your progress, not your notes. Where a card goes after you share it is decided by you and by whatever app you send it to.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app never sees your payment details. Apple gives the app a receipt confirming what you own, and that stays on your device.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything above. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing to ask the developer to erase.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change appears here and in the app release notes. A version of this app that started sending data anywhere would be a different app to the one described here.
